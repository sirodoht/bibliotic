# Bookshop data sources

## Additions checked on 15 November 2026

Addresses were checked against the linked shop or gallery pages (or the shop’s seller listing). Coordinates are point locations, not guarantees of an exact entrance. Names and house numbers were matched when geocoding; operator postcodes take precedence over postcode labels returned by map data. No opening-hours claims are stored.
